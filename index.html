Simple Text Filter

Send fundraising blasts, “quick survey” texts and other junk from numbers you don’t know straight to Junk — silently, using a keyword list you control.

  • Whole-word matching: “pac” catches “Super PAC” but not “package”.
  • Links count: “actblue” catches secure.actblue.com links.
  • A built-in Test box shows whether any text would be filtered.
  • Everything happens on your iPhone. No account, no servers, no data collected.
