Simple Text Filter

Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Short version: Simple Text Filter doesn’t collect, store, share or sell any of your data. Everything it does happens on your iPhone, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

What the app handles

Your keyword list

The words and phrases you add are stored only on your iPhone, in storage shared between the app and its message filter. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes the list.

Incoming text messages

When a text arrives from a number that isn’t in your contacts, iOS gives the sender and the message text to the app’s filter. The filter checks it against your keyword list on your iPhone and tells iOS whether to put it in Junk. It does not save, log or send the message anywhere.

Apple runs message filters in a restricted sandbox that cannot send data off your device. Simple Text Filter also never uses the optional server lookup that iOS offers to filter apps.

The Test box

Text you paste into the Test box is checked on your iPhone and is not saved.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising
  • No third-party code or SDKs
  • No servers — the app never connects to the internet

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
