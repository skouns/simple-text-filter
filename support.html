Support

Set it up

  1. Install Simple Text Filter and open it. It starts with a list of common fundraising words — add your own or swipe left on any word to remove it.
  2. Open Settings → Apps → Messages → Text Message Filter and choose Text Filter. If you can’t find it, search Settings for “Text Message Filter”.
  3. That’s it. Texts from unknown numbers that contain one of your words now go to Junk without a notification.

Common questions

A text still came through. Why?

iOS only passes texts (SMS, MMS and RCS) from numbers that aren’t in your contacts to filter apps. iMessages, texts from contacts, and texts from a number you’ve replied to several times are never checked. Also make sure the word is on your list exactly — plurals are separate words, so list both “donation” and “donations”.

Where do filtered texts go?

To the Spam or Junk list in Messages (the name depends on your iOS version). Open Messages and use the filter menu at the top of the conversation list to see them. Nothing is deleted.

Will I miss verification codes?

Only if the code’s text contains one of your words. Keep your list specific and use the Test box in the app to check a message before you rely on a new word.

Can the app delete texts?

No. iOS doesn’t let any app delete messages. Filtered texts go to Junk, where you can still read or delete them.

Does the app read my messages?

Only texts from unknown numbers, only to compare them with your list, and only on your iPhone. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.
